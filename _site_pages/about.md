Welcome to my personal site.

This blog is built with Jekyll and deployed via GitHub Actions. It supports LaTeX math with KaTeX and hosts small HTML mini-games.

## What you'll find here

- **Posts** — notes, tutorials, and ideas
- **Games** — browser-based mini-games and experiments
- **Math** — formulas rendered with KaTeX in posts

More sections (e.g. projects) can be added later by extending collections and the home layout.
